Subby is a minimalistic subscription dashboard for tracking monthly recurring expenses.

• Dashboard with monthly spend, yearly total, active count, biggest subscription, upcoming renewals (30 days), category pie and forecast
• Subscriptions list — sort by name/cost/renewal, filter active/archived/all, search, long-press actions (Edit / Archive / Delete)
• Subscription detail — hero tile, renewal countdown, monthly/yearly cost breakdown
• Add/Edit — name, amount, billing cycle, renewal date, category, icon + color, notes, validation
• Settings — theme (System/Light/Dark), currency (6), monthly budget, renewal reminders (local, day before ~09:00), password change, account deletion
• Offline-first — cached reads + FIFO write queue, syncs on reconnect
• Private — no ads, no tracking, data only email + your subscriptions + prefs; stored in Supabase (US) + on device

Permissions: Notifications (optional renewal reminders, POST_NOTIFICATIONS). No exact alarms, no location, no ads.
