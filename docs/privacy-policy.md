# Privacy Policy

**Subby** ("the App") is a subscription tracking application. This policy
explains what data the App processes and how.

## Data we collect

- **Account data**: when you create an account (email + password), we store
  your email address and a password hash for authentication. We never see or
  store your plaintext password.
- **Your subscriptions**: the subscription records you create (name, amount,
  currency, billing cycle, renewal date, category, notes).
- **Preferences**: your app preferences (currency, monthly budget, reminder
  setting) are associated with your account so they stay in sync across
  devices.
- **Device-local data**: scheduled reminder notifications and their IDs live
  only on your device and are never uploaded.
- **Purchase history**: if you upgrade to Pro, Apple/Google share purchase
  receipts (product + expiry + transaction ID) so we can verify your
  entitlement. We do not see your card details.

## How data is used

All data exists solely to provide the App's functionality: showing your
subscriptions, computing totals, and scheduling renewal reminders. We do not
sell data, show ads, or use your data for any purpose beyond running the App.

## Where data lives

Data is stored with our hosting provider (Supabase) in US-based infrastructure
and on your device. Authentication is handled by the same provider.

## Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the App (Settings → Account →
Delete account). This permanently removes your account, subscriptions, and
preferences from our servers. Device-local notification schedules are removed
as part of the app data on your device.

## Offline use

While offline, the App shows a locally cached copy of your data and queues
changes you make; they sync to the server when connectivity returns.
