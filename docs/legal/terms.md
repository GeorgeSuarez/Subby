## 1. The App

Subby is a subscription tracker provided as-is. Subby Pro is an optional paid upgrade.

## 2. Subscriptions

- **Monthly**: $2.99 per month, auto-renewable via App Store / Play Store.
- **Yearly**: $19.99 per year, auto-renewable. Includes a 7-day free trial on first purchase (one per account). If you do not cancel during the trial, you will be charged $19.99 when the trial ends. Yearly saves ~44% vs monthly.
- **Lifetime**: $49.99 one-time, non-consumable, no expiry.

Prices are shown in your local currency via the App Store / Play Store and may vary after conversion/tax.

### Auto-renewal

Subscriptions auto-renew at the end of each period (month/year) unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Your account will be charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the period. You can manage or cancel auto-renew in the App Store (Settings → Subscriptions) or Google Play (Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions). Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period.

### Free trial

The Yearly trial is one per Apple ID / Google account. Cancel anytime during the trial in the App Store / Play Store to avoid being charged. No charge if canceled before the trial ends.

### Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple (https://reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google (https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637) per their policies. We cannot directly refund store purchases.

### Restore Purchases

Use Settings → Restore Purchases or the paywall Restore link to restore entitlements on reinstall / new device (same Apple ID / Google account + same Subby account via `appAccountToken` mapping).
