FAQ

  • How do reminders work? Enable Settings → Reminders. Subby schedules a local notification the day before each renewal at ~09:00 (inexact, ±15 min).
  • Offline? Subby caches your data and queues changes when offline; they sync on reconnect.
  • Delete account? Settings → Account → Delete account, or see Delete Account.
