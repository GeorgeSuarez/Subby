Delete Your Subby Account

You can delete your account and all data at any time:

  1. Open Subby → Settings → Account → Delete account → Confirm.
  2. This permanently removes your account, subscriptions, and preferences from our servers (Supabase `auth.users` cascade deletes `subscriptions` and `user_prefs`). Local notification schedules are removed with app data.

Alternative: email georgesuarezdev@gmail.com from your account email with subject “Delete Subby Account”.

Data is deleted within 7 days, backups within 30 days.
